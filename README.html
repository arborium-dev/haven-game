NALOXONE

this is a game where drugs are illegal, and you do crimes.

After many years enlisted in the Korean army, you fell into a deep depression and cope with intense psychedelic drugs. After a bad trip, you now fight your own mind for a chance to escape by battling through your toughest trial yet, PING PONG.

Gameplay:

  • play ping pong
  • feel like you're on acid!
  • multiplayer!

Controls:

Player 1 (left side)

  • W / S - move up / down
  • Space - swing

Player 2 (right side) - untick P2 BOT on the menu to play local 2 player

  • Up / Down arrows - move up / down
  • Enter - swing

Menu

  • Space / Enter - start a round
  • Mouse - click the checkboxes to change options
  • after someone wins, press any key to go back to the menu

How to play:

  • hold up or down while you swing to aim your shot
  • the ring around your paddle is your hit zone: a ball inside the ring is a clean hit (fast and deep), a ball just outside it is a weak hit (slow and short)
  • hits fill up your special ring. clean hits fill it fast, weak hits barely fill it
  • when the ring turns purple, your next hit is a SUPER! move up, stay still, or move down while you hit it to send it to the top corner, the middle, or the bottom corner. supers make the ball faster for the rest of the point
  • too many weak hits and the ball won't make it over the net. it rolls back to you, so hit it again to save it!
  • first to 6 points wins

Menu options:

  • CUTSCENES - play the super cutscene (off = just a quick pause)
  • TUTORIAL - a quick tutorial before the round starts
  • P2 BOT - player 2 is a bot (untick for local 2 player)
  • SPLIT MUSIC - different songs for the tutorial, the game, after a super, and the win screen
  • HARD MODE - supers speed the ball up even more
